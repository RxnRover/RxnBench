# Spacing notes for SV-08 workspace

Notes created Aug 11, 2026

There are 8 zones in the current workspace designed around SOVOL SV08.

The total width is 350 x 350 mm

The center is then

(x,y) = (175,175)

The three main rows are 117 mm apart in the y direction (up and down)

The top row has enough space for 2 standardly oriented microplates
which are 192 mm apart (96 mm from center) in the x direction (left and right)

The middle row contains enough space for three microplates rotated 90 or 270 degrees and are 96 mm apart from eachother

The bottom row can either have 2 microplates in similiar orientation to the top row or, have three plates with two slimmer plates on the left and ride side and one microplate in the center in standard orientation.

It is important to note that the sizes documented in labware do not account for the size of the general purpose foot print. The footprint itself is roughly ~5mm offset from the plate itself. This means that there will appear to be a gap in the editor, though in reality the footprints are effectively touching eachother.

It also important to note that the footprint themselves are also 3mm tall (in the z direction) and the workspace itself is roughly 5mm tall in the z direction.
